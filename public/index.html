Upload photos of HDD/SSD labels. Serials are extracted automatically; drives that can't be decoded are flagged for a closer retake.

How to photograph drives for reliable results
  • Resolution rule: the serial barcode must span ≥ ~1000 px. A 2.5" SSD label fills that when the drive is ≥ ~3000 px wide in the photo.
  • 12 MP photo (≈4000×3000): 1 drive per photo, drive fills the frame width.
  • 50 MP photo (≈8160×6120): up to 4 drives (2×2), each filling a quarter of the frame.
  • Hold the camera parallel to the label, avoid glare/reflections, tap to focus on the label.
  • More drives per photo = smaller barcodes = slower scans and unreadable serials. When in doubt, shoot closer.
